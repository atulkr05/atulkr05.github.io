Research & Experience

My research lies at the intersection of Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and the development of Trustworthy AI systems. I focus on pioneering robust adversarial defenses to secure biometric data against unauthorized usage, advancing deep generative paradigms like Vision Transformers and Diffusion Models, and exploring Data & Machine Unlearning to build genuinely Responsible AI systems.
